## Interpretation
1. **Highest productivity:** Development has the highest average productivity, with 38.20 tasks per employee.
2. **Maximum tasks:** Indhu completed the highest number of tasks (48).
3. **Poor attendance:** Charan, Gokul have attendance below 75%, so the poor-attendance count is 2.
4. **Attendance and productivity:** The correlation coefficient is 0.82, showing a strong positive relationship in this dataset. It does not establish cause and effect, and individual cases vary.
5. **Suggestions:** Discuss attendance barriers with employees, set clear and realistic goals, provide training and mentoring, review workload distribution, recognize consistent performance, and monitor both task quality and quantity.

## Conclusion
Development records the highest average task completion in this sample. The analysis also identifies two employees below the attendance threshold. The positive association between attendance and completed tasks suggests that attendance may be one useful indicator to monitor, but it should be considered alongside workload, task difficulty, resources, and work quality.
